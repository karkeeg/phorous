02 — The Craft

Slow knit, because fast fabric never sits right.

Our cloth comes off circular loopwheel frames that turn roughly one metre an hour — a fiftieth of the pace of a modern knit. The yarn is never stretched, so the finished tee has no tension to release: it keeps its shape through a hundred washes and softens instead of shrinking.

Every body is cut by hand, sewn with twin-needle hems and finished with a self-fabric neck tape so the collar never ripples.
